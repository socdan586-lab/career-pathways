# Career Pathways

> Make your next career step easier to see.

## The Opportunity

Career pathways connect goals with the fields, capabilities, experiences, and projects that can move a person forward.

## Who It Serves

Students, career changers, working professionals, and mentors.

## What It Makes Possible

- Explore roles and fields through their real work
- Identify capabilities and learning priorities
- Turn career goals into practical next steps

## How People Use It

1. Choose a role, field, or direction to explore.
2. Review its capabilities and relevant experiences.
3. Select a learning or project step that creates evidence of progress.

## How It Connects

- CareerWBT pathways can draw on EruditeWBT learning resources and practical projects across the ecosystem.
